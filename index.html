The Coffee Realm

Ordering, loyalty and a back office for a homemade café and bakery in Quezon City.

Client projectWebsite + ordering system2026

Customers order on the website, track their order and collect stamps on a QR loyalty card. Staff run the shop from a back office with three roles, the kitchen sees every order on a tablet, and a partner's Android till stays in sync in real time, with an offline queue for when the wifi drops.

ONE UP

A fitness app that turns training into a game.

Client projectAndroid app2026

ONE UP logs workouts and meals, then pays you back in XP, weekly streaks and badges, with an AI coach on call. I joined in September 2026 as the main developer and built the rewards, the AI coach and the landing page. The app is in closed testing.
